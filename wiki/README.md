# wiki/ - the AI-maintained knowledge layer (the AI owns this)

The librarian writes and maintains everything here. Each page is a synthesized, source-cited
markdown note about one subject. You read it; you generally don't hand-edit it (if you do,
tell the AI so it doesn't overwrite you).

Subfolders:
- `entities/` - people, companies, tools, accounts
- `concepts/` - ideas, theses, frameworks
- `projects/` - active builds and engagements
- `decisions/` - dated calls with rationale
- `reference/` - durable facts, how-tos, account maps

The pages prefixed `example-` are demonstrations of the schema. Delete them once your own
wiki starts filling in - they exist so your vault is not blank on day one.
