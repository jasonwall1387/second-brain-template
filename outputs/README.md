# outputs/ - generated artifacts (the AI writes here on request)

When you ask the librarian for a substantial answer, report, or lint audit, it saves the
result here so you have a durable copy. Examples:
- `lint-2026-01-01.md` - a monthly wiki health report
- answers to big questions you asked via `/query`

Safe to prune anytime. Nothing here is a source of truth - it is derived from `wiki/` and
`raw/`.
